Pattern Detection Rule

SETU uses a simple threshold-based rule for the prototype.

Example:

If the same request occurs at least twice within five minutes, SETU can flag it as a pattern for caregiver attention.

SAME_REQUEST × 2 → within 5 minutes → PATTERN ALERT

Why SETU?

Designed around communication, context and caregiver awareness.

🔄 Bi-directional loop

SETU connects user communication with caregiver context, rather than stopping at symbol-to-speech communication.

🔔 Meaningful alerts

Threshold-based alerts reduce unnecessary notifications caused by individual taps.

🔒 Privacy by default

The prototype is designed around local-first communication data rather than unnecessary cloud storage.

♿ Accessibility

Large buttons, optional sound and calmer visual modes support different communication preferences.

📊 Context, not diagnosis

SETU highlights communication patterns for caregivers; it does not claim to diagnose medical conditions.

💡 Future-ready

The proof of concept can later be evaluated and expanded with clinicians, caregivers and users.
